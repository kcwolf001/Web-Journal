## Tips for Using These Tweets:

1. **Rotate through different angles** - Don't post similar tweets back-to-back
2. **Add visuals** - Screenshots of the beautiful UI will increase engagement
3. **Time strategically** - Post "morning pages" tweets in AM, reflection tweets in PM
4. **Use relevant hashtags** - #JournalingCommunity #WritingCommunity #ProductivityTwitter
5. **Engage with responses** - Reply to questions and feedback to build community
6. **A/B test** - Try different CTAs and see what resonates
7. **Share user stories** - If users share how WebJournal helped them, retweet/quote
8. **Pin your best performer** - Keep your highest-engagement tweet at the top of your profile
